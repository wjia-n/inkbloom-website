Move your entire library, without an account

Every reader eventually changes phones. InkBloom handles that without an account: you export one file, move it to the new device yourself, and import it there. That is the whole process.

Three steps

  1. On the old device: open Transfer and export your library to one .inkbloom file.
  2. Move that file to the new device however you like.
  3. On the new device: open Transfer and import the file. Your library, progress and notes come back exactly.

What the file carries

  • Your books, exactly as you imported them, with covers.
  • Your highlights, notes and bookmarks.
  • Your reading progress, including your exact position in each book.
  • Your shelves, favourites, tags and reading stats.

One honest warning

Keep the file private. A .inkbloom file is a plain ZIP, not encrypted. Move it the way you would move the books themselves: USB, nearby share, a memory card, or any transfer you trust. It never touches a server.
