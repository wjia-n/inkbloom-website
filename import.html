Import your books

Importing means picking files on your device and letting InkBloom read them. Files are parsed, indexed and stored directly on your device. Zero bytes of your books ever leave the device.

Formats at a glance

FormatWhat happensNote
EPUBReflowable text, table of contents, covers, inline illustrations.Best format for most books.
PDFText extracted on your device.Scanned, image-only PDFs are rejected with a clear message; PDFs whose fonts carry no usable character mapping cannot be decoded.
TXT, Markdown, HTMLPlain and formatted text.Imports as-is.
FB2, RTF, DOCX, ODTCommon ebook and document formats.Converted on your device.
MOBI, AZWNot supported.Convert to EPUB first, then import.

How import works on each platform

  1. Android: pick files one by one or in batches, or scan a folder on your phone (up to 200 files).
  2. Windows and Linux: pick files from your computer into the desktop app.
  3. Web: pick files in your browser. Browsers cannot list a folder, so on the web you pick the files themselves; they are then saved in your browser’s storage.
