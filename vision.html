Our Vision

Reading is one of the quietest things a person does. Our vision is that the tool you read with should be just as quiet: no account standing between you and your books, no cloud keeping a copy, no feed competing for your attention, and no record of what you read kept anywhere but your own device.

lock

Your library is yours

Books you imported stay on hardware you control, and move with you when you change devices, without asking a company for permission.

spa

Calm is a feature

A reader should show the page, not notifications, guilt-driven streaks, or recommendations designed to sell you more.

devices

One reader, everywhere

The same offline reader on your phone, your computer and your browser, with the same books and the same exact place in each one.

verified

Honesty over growth

If a format cannot be read properly, InkBloom says so instead of pretending. If a feature does not exist yet, it is not advertised.

Where InkBloom is going

The direction is steady, not flashy: the same reader on more platforms, the same offline model, better handling of difficult files, and nothing that turns your reading into somebody else's data. New versions keep your library intact, with no manual backup step required.
