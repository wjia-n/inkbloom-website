What InkBloom is

InkBloom Reader is free software provided as-is. It is a reader for books you already own or have the right to read. It is not a bookstore, it does not sell or supply books, and it does not check or guarantee the content of any file you import.

Your books remain yours

You keep every right in the books and files you import. InkBloom claims no ownership over them, copies them nowhere, and cannot see them. The .inkbloom transfer file you export is yours to keep, move or delete.

Fair use
