Privacy Policy

The short version: InkBloom collects nothing about you. No account, no tracking, no ads in the app, no server holding your books. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

The app collects nothing

  • No account and no sign-in, so there is no identity to collect.
  • No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporters in the app.
  • No purchases, so there are no payment details anywhere.
  • Your books, covers, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading progress, stats, shelves and settings are stored only on your device: in the app's storage on Android, Windows and Linux, and in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on the web.
  • The app makes no network requests for your books or your reading. If you are offline, everything works exactly the same.

The .inkbloom transfer file

When you export your library, the app writes one .inkbloom file: a plain ZIP containing a library list, your books and your covers. It is not encrypted. The file goes wherever you choose to put it, and only you move it. InkBloom never sees it, stores it, or transmits it.

This website

  • This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP addresses) when pages are served, under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and styling from a CDN. Those providers may log technical data when their files are requested, under their own policies. The website itself sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.
  • If you set a profile name and photo using the profile button, they are saved only in your own browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and removing them deletes them from that browser.
  • The contact form on this site does not submit anything to a server; it opens your own email app.

Children

InkBloom collects no personal data from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal data at all. There is nothing to request, correct or delete on a server, because no server holds anything. To delete everything the app knows, uninstall the app (on a phone or computer) or clear the site's storage in your browser (on the web).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page changes with it, and the date above changes too. Questions: games@wajiha.fun.
