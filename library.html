library_books

Your library, on your device

Your library is the list of books you have imported into InkBloom. It lives only on your device: in the app's own storage on Android, Windows and Linux, and in your browser's storage when you read on the web. Nothing about your library is sent to a server, because there is no server.

shelves

Shelves you make

Group books any way you like: by mood, by project, by person. A book can sit on more than one shelf.

star

Favourites and tags

Star the books you love and tag the rest, so a big library stays findable without folders.

search

Search and sort

Search across titles and authors, and sort by most recent, title or author.

history

Last read, first

Your reading position in every book is kept, so the last book you were reading is always at the top.

Starting out

Three short sample books written for InkBloom are included so you can try the reader before importing your own. The moment you import your own books, the samples step aside and only your books are shown. This website cannot show your books, because it never sees them.
